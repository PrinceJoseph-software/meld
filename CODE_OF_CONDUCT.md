# Code of conduct

Meld follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults, discrimination and publishing others' private information are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

**Reporting.** If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/PrinceJoseph-software). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove content or block participants who break this code.
